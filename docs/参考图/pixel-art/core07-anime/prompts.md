## 第一版提示词

Create a polished original anime science-fiction puzzle videogame art-direction storyboard, a large wide 16:9 image with FOUR equally sized 16:9 gameplay screens arranged in a clean 2 by 2 grid. This is for CORE-07 / LAST SHUTTLE, a story-driven cooperative falling-block game about maintaining a failing orbital station long enough for the last evacuation shuttle to depart. The existing design was criticized as too much text, too few graphics, ugly metallic blocks, and insufficient visual storytelling or urgency. Solve those problems through actual compelling anime environmental illustration.

ART DIRECTION: bright, emotionally engaging Japanese anime sci-fi, clean cel shading, beautiful hand-painted animation-background feeling, airy whites and cream, strong cyan and cobalt, lavender shadows, coral and warm amber highlights. Visually designed as an attractive shipped game, not a wireframe, not a diagram, not NASA photorealism, not a grey industrial dashboard. Stylish illustrated orbital hangar with a huge observation window looking across a sunlit planet limb, an evacuation boarding gantry, a recognizable white/cyan rescue shuttle, and large expressive energy-transfer mechanisms. No existing franchise characters, logos, or copied UI. A small original adult anime pilot with short dark-blue hair and an ivory/cyan flight jacket can appear only as a small temporary radio portrait in the last screen, never dominating the puzzle.

COMPOSITION IN ALL FOUR SCREENS: same fixed camera, same central single shared WIDE falling-block playfield (approximately 24 columns by 20 rows, one board, never two separate boards) occupying about 45 percent of screen area. The rest of the screen is richly illustrated story environment, readily visible to both left and right of the playfield. The playfield has a quiet indigo/cobalt interior and a delicate clean white/cyan frame. Bottom quarter contains neatly stacked connected colorful square tetrominoes and one falling piece above, with a subtle outline-only ghost. Blocks are vivid solid cel-shaded pastel-jewel squares with a slim colored edge and one small broad corner shine; flat clean faces, NO grey metal casings, screws, inset sockets, big center holes, or thick borders. Preserve excellent puzzle readability. Tiny icon-based NEXT and HOLD previews close to the board. A simple four-icon progress trail replaces all task lists. Most interface panels from a dashboard have been removed. Do not fill blank areas with data. Show the story through people, shuttle, doors, power, and danger.

THE FOUR DISTINCT STORY CHAPTERS:
TOP LEFT, 01 REBOOT: bright cool-blue reserve-lit maintenance bay. A huge segmented hangar shutter is partly closed, large power conduits are visibly broken/disconnected and a dormant energy ring is shut. Through the remaining view, the evacuation shuttle is visibly docked and waiting. A few tiny stylized passengers wait behind an unpowered gantry gate. Localized amber alert lights and a leaking conduit clearly communicate trouble, while the overall art stays bright and inviting.
TOP RIGHT, 02 BOARDING: the enormous shutter is now OPEN, exposing much more of the planet and shuttle; the boarding gantry is fully extended and brilliantly illuminated. Several tiny readable passenger silhouettes are RUNNING across the bridge toward the shuttle. Energized cyan guide-light paths clearly show what the player's success has restored. This must be a major change of silhouettes and geometry, not merely a color swap.
BOTTOM LEFT, 03 IGNITION: the shuttle is still docked and boarding is nearly complete. A LARGE energy collar in the environment has physically UNFOLDED into an impressive open array; power is pouring through its cyan arcs toward the shuttle. Engine compartments open and nozzles begin to glow. More visible power leaks, steam, local sparks and warning lights make the emergency noticeably more serious. Players can immediately see this is a new phase even if all text is hidden.
BOTTOM RIGHT, 04 FINAL TRANSFER: the boarding bridge is retracted or closing after passengers are safely aboard; the recognizable shuttle is STILL DOCKED, two clamp arms are beginning their release sequence but it has not departed before victory. Its engines blaze with cyan light. Large power streams, localized coral/red rotating beacon reflections, a visibly damaged station spar, a few exterior debris silhouettes and strong diagonals create an urgent climax. The huge environmental energy-transfer apparatus is fully active. The puzzle board remains steady, clean, readable, with no obscuring effects. A very small pilot radio portrait and tiny waveform can express human stakes without a speech paragraph.

TEXT: very little, only English. Each screen gets just its small exact chapter label ('01 REBOOT', '02 BOARDING', '03 IGNITION', '04 FINAL TRANSFER') and small 'NEXT' / 'HOLD' labels if useful. Optionally one compact numeric objective such as '18/32'. Absolutely NO long mission descriptions, equipment explanations, blocks of captions, dialogue paragraphs, decorative microtext, big headings, or explanatory legend. Do not add prose outside the four screens. The scene must communicate the narrative and escalating emergency visually.
The final result should feel like a beautiful cohesive anime puzzle game's four gameplay screenshots, with four very obvious environmental states and restrained UI.
